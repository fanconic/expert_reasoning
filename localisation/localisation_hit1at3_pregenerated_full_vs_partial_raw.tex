\textbf{Localisation performance.} Hit@1@w is the fraction of traces where the predicted failure point (largest one-step reward drop on the corrupted trace) falls within \(\pm w\) tokens of the injected perturbation. We interpret performance via \(\Delta\) above chance (blue bracketed arrows): all models are substantially above random for dense H@1@1 and H@1@7, and remain clearly above random for interval (e.g., \(+13.2\), \(+30.1\), \(+26.0\) points). This indicates that reward drops are consistently close to the true corrupted step; qualitative trajectory examples are shown in Appendix~\ref{app:error_localisation_app}.
